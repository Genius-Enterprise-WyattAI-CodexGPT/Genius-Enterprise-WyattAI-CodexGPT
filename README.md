### What I'm building

A privacy-conscious platform in **pre-production development** for travel and event hospitality professionals. Its planned workflows cover work assignments, income, expenses, mileage, receipts, and operational organization.

### Approach

- **Operational clarity:** Design around the realities of live events and mobile work.
- **Privacy by design:** Keep personal information and business systems appropriately separated.
- **Human oversight:** Put meaningful decisions and approvals in the hands of people.
- **Cross-platform foundations:** Build with mobile and desktop experiences in mind.
